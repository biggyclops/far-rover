# Credits

Far Rover orbital board uses public-domain NASA imagery.

- **Orbital terrain:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / University of Arizona, HiRISE PIA23289, “Landing in Oxia Palus” (Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter). Public domain.
- **Rover cam:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / ASU / MSSS, Perseverance Mastcam-Z PIA23727, Navcam PIA24422, and Mastcam-Z/Navcam landscape PIA24543. Public domain.

Processed (luminance stretch, tan tint, landing blast, boulder dots) to match the orbital-ops concept. F8 and C10 are warped onto real HiRISE crater bowls.
