# Credits

Far Rover orbital board uses public-domain NASA imagery.

- **Orbital terrain:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / University of Arizona, HiRISE PIA23289, “Landing in Oxia Palus” (Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter). Public domain.
- **Rover cam:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / ASU / MSSS, Perseverance Mastcam-Z PIA23727, Navcam PIA24422, and Mastcam-Z/Navcam landscape PIA24543. Public domain.
- **Rover-cam crater plate:** NASA / JPL-Caltech / University of Arizona, HiRISE PIA08813 (young fresh crater), perspective-graded to the Mastcam-Z plate. Public domain.
- **Rover-cam lander:** NASA / JPL-Caltech, InSight first selfie PIA22876, cut out and graded to the Mastcam-Z plate. Public domain.

Processed (luminance stretch, tan tint, landing blast, boulder dots) to match the orbital-ops concept. F8 and C10 are warped onto real HiRISE crater bowls.

- **v0.8 surface ground:** NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, LROC NAC NAC_POLE_SOUTH_CM_345_P892S1350 (1 m/px polar stereographic, tile centred 89.2S 135.0E). Public domain. Citation: Wagner, R. V., Speyerer, E. J., Robinson, M. S., and LROC Team (2015), New Mosaicked Data Products from the LROC Team, LPSC 46, abstract #1473.
- **v0.8 rover cam (scout overlay):** NASA Apollo 15 Hasselblad AS15-82-11057, Hadley–Apennine looking toward the LM. Public domain. Greyscale crop to match the LROC plate. Root demo still uses Mastcam-Z.
