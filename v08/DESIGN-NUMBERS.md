Rim solar 30 reg, 10s, +5 power/s day, 0 night. Storage 30 reg, 10s, holds 100 ice + 200 reg. Printer 40 reg, 15s; prints hauler 25 reg + 10 power, 30s. Rising print cost (+10 reg per extra printed hauler) was tried and dropped: print-1 already beats two starters at ring 9, and +10 does not put the 2-digger tunnel in the 20–35% band.
Tunnel: hub 20 reg, 10s each, ≥6 tiles apart; a hub may sit next to Deep Ice after unlock. Dig 15 s/tile per rover (rovers stack; was 16, r4), 3 power/tile, yields 2 reg/tile; cargo hub-to-hub at 4 tiles/s, no rover. Vault 40 reg, holds 150 ice underground. A tunnel is one straight row or column that orthogonally touches both hubs — no diagonals, no corner-only tiles.
Goal: 180 ice + 50 power in storage at once -> "Land crew" button. Win screen: time, logistics (haulers only vs tunnel), #Notifies, #patches.
